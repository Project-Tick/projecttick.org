Hey! It’s been a while not too long, but still. I want to make a fresh start. That’s why we’re rebuilding MeshMC!

MultiMC has become active again—perhaps you’ve heard. Since MeshMC has also switched from the GPL to the Apache-2.0 license, I figured there would no longer be any obstacles to incorporating new commits. For this reason, I’d like to make a fresh start.

At the same time, an announcement.

I would like to declare that I will henceforth license all of my own contributions under the Microsoft Public License.

However, I will not release a stable version until the feature set is nearly on par with version 11.0.0. I plan to integrate and use MultiMC’s new updater. Thanks!
