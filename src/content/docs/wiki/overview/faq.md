## What is MeshMC?

MeshMC is a fork of MultiMC that retains the sustainability principle but also preserves its simplicity and allows for redistribution. Although it was forked in 2025, development began using an output from 2022.

The original fork was primarily a result of continuing disagreements between a portion of the community, and MultiMC's lead maintainer, mainly surrounding the topics of 3rd party packaging and re-distribution. Many users of the time, especially across Linux, and other Unix-like platforms, were left dissatisfied by MultiMC's policy of restricting self-built packages, by not allowing the launcher to build with the necessary API keys for the successful authentication of Microsoft Accounts.

## What does this mean for the future?

MeshMC will continue to have the same functionality as MultiMC, and we will continue to add features from there.

MeshMC will remain accessible to everyone across all platforms, so no one will be left behind. We do this by officially maintaining packages on a wide variety of operating systems, both natively and via Flatpak and AppImage releases on Windows, macOS, and Linux. And when there are gaps in the official distribution, third-party builds will always be welcome in our community.

Furthermore, while the MultiMC project focuses on "predictability" without "fancy features," MeshMC will strike the middle ground, with many new and exciting features on the horizon, but with predictability as the priority.
