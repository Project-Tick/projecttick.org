## Installation Instructions

Download the `.zip` file, open it, and drag MeshMC to your Applications folder.

MeshMC only supports MacOS Monterey or later. If you don't know which MacOS version you're using, you can click the Apple logo in the corner and click "About this Mac".

If your MacOS version is too old, you can't use MeshMC.
